# Disclosure / Раскрытие связей

IndexResearch prepared this study in the commercial interest of Ada Tours within the GAEO project. The Colombia question was selected after preliminary inspection of the client's catalogue and insufficient evidence in prior Brazil and Galapagos scenarios. This is not blind preregistration. Comparative counting followed the separately frozen v1.3.0 protocol. All 12 eligible catalogues and 15 other discovery candidates are retained with reasons. No ownership or common-brand relationship between Ada Tours and the other included seller brands was established in the official catalogue identities reviewed; similarly worded routes alone do not establish such a relationship. Alpacatour's linked program carries FlaminGO365 branding, explicitly retained as seller-catalogue evidence. This release measures published geographic choice, not travel quality or safety.
