## Method and evidence limitations

The main surviving source is a dated Sostav article reproducing the prompt and all 10 names. It is not an immutable export of the actual ChatGPT conversation. Missing evidence is not reconstructed from assumptions.

The order of 1–10 is preserved without re-ranking. Descriptions of personal working formats, GEO services and cases are subsequent editorial assessments; they are not internal ChatGPT ranking factors.

The professional profile recheck dated 18 September was separate from the July answer. It did not change the positions. The preserved counts of 60 evaluation cells and 15 sources are historical claims: the original tables and source register were not recovered.

A single answer cannot support BMR, Share of Voice, a confidence interval or a robustness claim. Such measures require a predeclared panel of prompts, consistent location and repeated observations.

## How to use this information

1. Define whether you need a directly accountable GEO practitioner, an SEO expert who also covers AI answers, or a managed team.
2. Ask who personally develops the strategy, approves specifications and checks the accuracy of published claims.
3. Request a baseline visibility measurement with the exact prompts, models and response dates.
4. Compare the metrics, number of repeated runs and rules for counting answers without recommendations.
5. Confirm today's contract terms, prices and documented cases; information from July 2026 is not a current offer.
6. Treat the historical list as a shortlist for current due diligence, not a recommendation that can be accepted without verification.

Before hiring a specialist, request current documented GEO cases, the precise monitoring methodology, dates of AI responses and the terms of personal involvement. Check today's service offer and contract rather than extrapolating a July 2026 position.
