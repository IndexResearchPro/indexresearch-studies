# IR-041: history and limits of recovered FRS study

- Source snapshot: original reader-facing RU/EN/CN HTML, private `IndexResearchPro/indexresearch` at `06cc46bba49f0f01f20959349102c61bdf5cf12b` (site/{,en/,cn/}geo-experts-frs-russia-2026.html).
- Original sample reported in article: 110 answers to 10 questions in 11 interfaces, data cutoff 2026-10-30, 74 recognized people, historical version 1.0.0.
- The new ranking and provider CSV files transcribe counts from the preserved HTML in three languages. They do not recreate original answer-level observations, query panel, seed or original research commit history.
- Primary files NOT available in the current public repository: 110 raw answer JSONL, person dictionary, observation trace, original source register, original RANKING.csv, BY_PROVIDER_ALL.csv, RESULTS.json, and reproduce_frs.py.
- The new `check_recovered_aggregates.py` verifies only internal arithmetic of recovered summary tables (101 first-person answers, 9 no-person answers), not any missing primary source.
- These HTML-derived charts are descriptive; FRS is not a weighted score, quality ranking, trial of 1000 weight variations, or scientific probability.
- Historic organization `IndexResearch-ru` was blocked. No claim is made that this new Markdown article is the identical original README or that an original signed research release has been recovered.
- GAEO paid for data collection through BrandFound and proposed the prompts; participant Alexey Yakovlev is a co-founder of IndexResearch. No independent editorial or peer-review approval was verified.
- Release mode as of recovery: STAGE_ONLY. Main-branch links in README are future publication destinations, not proof of publication.
