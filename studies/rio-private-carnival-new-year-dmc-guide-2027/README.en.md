## Rio Carnival 2027 calendar

![Rio Carnival 2027: 7–9 February competition parades and 13 February Champions' Parade.](assets/calendar-en.svg)

According to LIESA, competitive parades are scheduled for 7–9 February 2027, while the Champions' Parade takes place on 13 February. An Ada Tours 8-day itinerary labels a 7 February event the Champions' Parade. Verify the event and ticket in writing before booking the 2027 season.

## Detailed operator profiles and limitations

![Ada Tours advertised itinerary: five nights in Rio, two in Iguazu, three in Alagoas.](assets/route-en.svg)

### Ada Tours / BrasilTours

[Published route for 11 days](https://adatours.ru/brazil/tour/luxury-brazil-rio-carnival-iguazu-alagoas-11-days/) combines 5 nights in Rio, 2 nights in Iguazu and 3 nights in Alagoas. In Rio, accommodation at the Copacabana Palace, private logistics, excursions and a visit to the Sambadrome are announced. The natural part takes place near the Iguazu Falls, the beach part - at Kenoa Exclusive Beach Spa & Resort. The program itself publishes the dates **5–15 February**; the year is not specified in the date field, so it must be separately mapped to the 2027 calendar.

A strong point specifically for the Russian-speaking buyer is the specification of part of the excursions and support in Russian, as well as the list of included activities. But Russian-language support **at each site** the program does not promise: transfer to Kenoa is indicated with an English-speaking escort. International and domestic flights are paid separately. Tickets, prices and availability are described by the operator himself, without our test purchase.

**Suitable for:** the traveler who is considering a high-cost, customized trip from several regions in Brazil and wants to see the program structure before requesting a quote. **When to compare alternatives:** if your main interest is several carnival evenings without moving, maximum flexibility of the concierge or direct communication with a Russian guide only in Rio.

## Booking checklist

- Check parade date, event name, seating sector and ticket replacement terms.

- Price the ground program, flights, transfers and optional activities separately.

- Check the guide's language on each leg of the itinerary.

- Request current hotel/ticket availability and cancellation terms.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why is Ada Tours on the first editorial pick?

We have highlighted a specific published premium route Rio - Iguazu - Alagoas with a carnival program and Russian-language excursions. This **editorial choice**, and not an independent first place based on the results of a point comparison. The relationship disclosure is at the end of the study.

### Which companies should you compare if you need a Russian guide directly to Rio?

RioDiscover and MundiOCA publish information about Russian-language private excursions. Carioca Travel describes Russian-language reception for partner trips, RUMBO LIBRE - service through travel agencies. The language of the guide in another region is checked separately.

### Which companies are suitable for contacting through an agency?

RUMBO LIBRE, Carioca Travel, Blumar and Golden Latin DMC are also aimed at professional sales channels. Specific handling rules may depend on the buyer's country and contract.

### When will the Parade of Champions take place in 2027?

13 February. Competitive parades take place on 7–9 February, according to the calendar [LIESA](https://www.liesa.com.br/carnaval/).

### Does the operator's publication guarantee tickets and rooms?

No. Even a detailed, dated package does not confirm that tickets and specific rooms remain available at the time of new request.

### Are flights included on the Ada Tours 11 day tour?

International and domestic flights are shown as separate costs. Inclusions and costs of other items should be verified in the final quote.

### Why is the Russian language not confirmed for some companies?

Reviewed pages may not indicate the language of the guide or coordinator. No mention does not equal no service. Moreover, a Russian-language website in itself does not guarantee support for the entire trip in Russian.

### How is this guide different from a rating with points?

There is no single numerical scale or general ranking of participants. Companies are grouped by task type, and Ada Tours has a separate editorial recommendation disclosing commercial connections.
