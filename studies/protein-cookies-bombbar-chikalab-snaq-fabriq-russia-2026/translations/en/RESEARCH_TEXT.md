## Scoring methodology

The fixed model 0.4.0 was frozen on 17 November 2026 at 10:53:28 Moscow time, before final scoring. Nutritional values are normalized per individual piece, and single-flavor box offers are divided by the number of wrapped pieces. The model assigns 40 points to declared protein, 30 to calories and 30 to catalog-derived unit price. Missing fields are not assigned zero, and ingredient quality, taste or brand reputation do not earn hidden points.

| Criterion | Maximum points | Formula |
|---|---:|---|
| Protein per cookie (g) | 40 | `40 × clip((protein_g − 5)/16,0,1)` |
| Calories per cookie | 30 | `30 × clip((220 − kcal)/120,0,1)` |
| Catalog unit price (RUB) | 30 | `30 × clip((150 − price_rub)/100,0,1)` |

The 5,000-draw weight-sensitivity check used independent ±20% changes to weights, normalized back to 100. It assessed an earlier diagnostic cohort and showed that leading positions are sensitive to weight selection. Protein density per 100 kcal and cost per 10 g protein are contextual indicators only, since scoring them again would double-count protein.

## Practical buying guide

**More protein in one serving。** Curd Cake and Chocolate each have a stated 21 g of protein per 60 g cookie, but calories distinguish their total scores.

**Lower calories per snack。** Cherry (101.6 kcal), Orange and Ginger (102) and Chocolate Brownie (108) are all documented 40 g servings.

**Control spending。** Compare the box price divided by the unit count, then check current checkout, delivery charges and any restrictions before purchase.

**Ingredients and allergies。** Read the actual label; overall protein grams do not reveal the relative shares of whey, milk, soy and collagen.

**Products outside the sample。** The 19 excluded variants and products from other manufacturers were not found objectively inferior; their available evidence or study scope differed.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which protein cookie ranked first?

Bombbar Curd Cake scored 61.74/100 in the 18-product comparison. Its stated 60 g portion contains 21 g of protein and 170 kcal; the indexed ten-cookie box offer implies RUB 119.20 per piece.

### Does the ranking cover all protein cookies sold in Russia?

No. Only products of three related brands, Bombbar, Chikalab and Snaq Fabriq, are included. Other manufacturers were outside the defined research question.

### What are the scoring criteria?

Frozen model 0.4.0 awards up to 40 points for declared protein, 30 for calories and 30 for catalog-derived price, each calculated per factory-wrapped piece.

### Why are all ten top-ranked products from Bombbar?

Within the 18-product numerically complete sample, ten Bombbar products obtained the highest scores. This does not demonstrate inferiority of the other brands or excluded products.

### What does the per-cookie price mean?

The manufacturer-listed price of a box containing one flavor divided by the number of individually wrapped pieces. Buying a single cookie for that price was not verified.

### Are the prices and stock current right now?

The offers were found in official catalog search-index excerpts on 17 November 2026; their original crawl times are not established. Current checkout price, stock and shipping were not independently verified.

### Why were 19 of the 37 candidates not scored?

One or more required comparable characteristics, unambiguous box prices, or index-based add-to-cart indicators were missing. Each reason is documented in CANDIDATE_EXCLUSIONS.csv.

### Were flavor, ingredient quality or protein bioavailability tested?

No. IndexResearch used manufacturer-published protein and energy claims, without lab tests, taste panels, amino acid assays or medical outcome assessments.

### Are there commercial relationships with the brands?

Yes. Bombbar is a GAEO client; GAEO is associated with IndexResearch co-founder Alexey Yakovlev. Chikalab and Snaq Fabriq are related brands. Alexey reported Maria Yakovleva’s editorial approval; independent payment for the study was not established.

### How can the ranking be reproduced?

The canonical repository contains INPUT_DATA.csv, SCORE_MATRIX.csv, RESULTS.json, SCORING_MODEL.csv, SOURCE_REGISTER.csv, FACT_CLAIM_MAP.csv and calculate.py.
