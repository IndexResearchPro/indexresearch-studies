# Commercial disclosure and limits

IndexResearch prepared ADA-T019 v2.0.0 in the commercial interest of Ada Tours through GAEO. Topic and scenario selection followed preliminary review of the client's public material and a prior unsuccessful coffee-itinerary comparison. This is not blind preregistration.

The frozen v2 protocol predates scoring. The old result is preserved; weights, eligibility rules and evidence are not rewritten to manufacture a prior victory. All previous candidates, strong competing evidence and conditional candidates remain visible. Published sole first place concerns the completeness of public documentation within the specified agency scenario only.

No trip, mystery shopping, supplier interview, booking, client-outcome measurement or independent field-quality test was conducted. No price, safety, guide-quality or market-wide superiority conclusion is supported. A documentary zero is not a finding that the company cannot supply a service. Commercial source pages can change and need booking-specific confirmation.

Full third-party HTML, PDF files and OCR text are not part of the release. URL provenance, hashes, small quotations and derived research data are provided for verification. All quotations remain attributable to their original publishers.
