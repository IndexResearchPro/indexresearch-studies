# Editorial recovery record

This research is a text-first restoration of the previously published 2026 personal GEO specialist comparison, historical methodology 2.0.0, data cutoff 2026-09-16.

The old GitHub repository and its scoring matrix, original primary-source register, RESULTS.json, protocol and executable validation could not be located in the current public archive. Numeric standings and methodological weights were copied from the three preserved HTML editions in the private site repository. The three localized SVG diagrams in assets/ are representations of these published totals and weights, not fresh data collection.

The content was prepared on an editorial task branch pending the separate consolidated release. This note does not claim that the original computations are independently verifiable.
