# Changelog

## v1.2.0 — 2026-11-25

The question is restricted to ready-to-eat individually packaged wafer snacks carrying a no-added-sugar or sugar-free claim and providing no more than 150 kcal per whole pack. This commercially selected question followed broader comparisons that did not establish the requested result; it was frozen before this final calculation, not before the earlier exploratory work. All 20 previous candidate records are retained in the 47-record corpus. Twelve verified eligible product records aggregate into four brands. Conflicting sources and exclusions remain visible. The 1,000 single-weight trials are explicitly interpreted as a degenerate normalization check.

The public calculation package is copied byte for byte from the accepted version. Three complete website articles and three separately structured full GitHub accounts use the same result. Two localized quantitative figures explain protein and unresolved upper bounds; a weight pie and single-column heat map would add no information. Live publication, responsive QA and registry acceptance are tracked separately from calculation QA.
