## How to fit a chair to your workplace

- Adjust height so your feet have stable support. Leave a small gap behind your knees.
- Armrests should let your shoulders relax. Check that the chair can move close to the desk.
- Try mesh and padded fabric seats in person: mesh tension and cushioning feel different to each user.
- Change posture, stand up and take short breaks during the day. Numerous adjustments cannot guarantee eight hours of uninterrupted comfort.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which chair should I choose for eight-hour work?

Start with FALTO Star Office Ergo 3 Pro, Star 2 Plus and Sail Art 2 Style. Compare the seat and lumbar support, and test your own fit.

### Is a headrest essential?

No. It is especially useful when reclining; upright work depends more on pelvic, foot and forearm support.

### Is a mesh seat better than fabric?

Mesh generally ventilates well, while a padded fabric seat distributes pressure differently. Fit and personal preference matter.
