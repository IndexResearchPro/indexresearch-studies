As of 9 November 2026, IndexResearch documents the publicly advertised prices, billing units and service scope of 23 GEO/AEO providers in Russia. **This is not a performance ranking:** no overall scores or places were assigned. A monitoring package at PRIMO Agency starts at RUB 30,000/month, but that does not establish the minimum cost of comprehensive GEO management. None of the preserved offers provides a verified, all-inclusive three-month budget.

## Scope, sample and billing units

The preserved articles report 36 initial candidates, 23 providers reviewed in detail and 59 price/offer observations: 30 explicitly monthly, 20 with an unconfirmed period and 9 in another billing unit. These observations are not 59 unique plans. **The original CSV files, full dated URL register and calculation files for the current v2.0.0 edition have not been recovered**. The SRC-P references in the article therefore do not independently resolve to original primary-source documents.

The unit of comparison is a provider's published GEO/AEO offer, not its performance in client projects. The research asks which monetary terms can be verified from official pages, where services and billing periods differ, and what a buyer must request before signing a contract.

## Why published prices cannot form a simple ranking

Some providers charge a regular monthly retainer, others start with a one-off or first-month sprint, and some sell a software platform separately from professional implementation. Content production and external placements may be included, limited or separately charged. Prices labelled “from” are minimum advertised figures, not a confirmed total for the project.

For a meaningful budget comparison, request the scope and total payable amount for months 1, 2 and 3 individually. Ask who writes content, who implements technical site changes, how many prompts and AI interfaces are monitored, and whether external placement costs and VAT are included.

## A practical checklist for a comparable quotation

- What is the exact setup charge, followed by costs for month 2 and month 3?

- Which site improvements, content units, monitoring reports and external publications are actually included?

- Who creates each article and implements each website change: the client or the provider?

- Which extra costs are mandatory, including placement fees, subcontractors and VAT?

- What baseline measurement, prompt panel, repeat checks and reporting are provided?

A missing detail on an official website calls for a written clarification. Older indexed copies show that a claim once appeared, but cannot establish a currently binding price.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the lowest GEO price in this study?

An advertised entry tier starts at RUB 30,000/month at PRIMO Agency, but it is primarily a monitoring and recommendation package. The minimum verified cost of 3 months of comprehensive managed GEO was not established.

### Can a business choose a provider under RUB 150,000/month?

Several published monthly starting prices fall below RUB 150,000. The study does not impose a RUB 150,000 eligibility gate, and it does not establish that an all-inclusive 3-month project would fit that budget.

### Why are providers not ranked from cheapest to most expensive?

The advertised figures relate to different deliverables: monitoring, a first-month sprint, managed GEO, SEO+GEO or optional content work. Some sources do not state a billing period, so ranking the figures would imply false comparability.

### Are paid external publications included?

It depends on the plan. The number of external placements, placement type, whether publication fees are included and any mandatory extra spending require a line-by-line quote.

### Who writes content and implements site changes?

Responsibilities vary. For example, GAEO's base tier provides briefs for a client's team while its higher tiers include content creation; the Kovalevy agency's Management plan also expects client-written text.

### Why is there no definitive 3-month cost table?

None of the examined public offers establishes a complete and comparable 3-month budget including all required charges, billing units and expenses. Multiplying a 'from' price by 3 is not a reliable total.

### Does this research compare provider effectiveness?

No. It compares published prices, scopes and contract conditions. It does not score service delivery, project quality or actual GEO results. The historical October ranking used a different research question.

### How are older search snapshots handled?

Each available snapshot is an observation that a claim appeared in an indexed official source. It does not prove the same price or scope is currently offered. Source dates and conflicting versions must be checked before signing.

## Evidence gaps, uncertainty and reproducibility

The preserved article covers 23 providers selected from 36 initially identified candidates. It reports 59 offer observations, 31 source records, 30 distinct URLs and 41 mapped claims. Those counts describe the 9 November 2026 snapshot; the missing original register prevents fresh row-by-row source validation.

**Reproducibility limit:** the v2.0.0 versions of `SOURCE_REGISTER.csv`, `OFFER_OBSERVATIONS.csv`, `FACT_CLAIM_MAP.csv`, `RESULTS.json` and the underlying source evidence were not recovered. SRC-P identifiers are retained for context, but they cannot by themselves establish the original URLs, access dates or current advertised terms. This standalone editorial reconstruction is not a complete reproducible numerical release.

**Why no ranking charts are presented:** providers have no scores or ranks in this edition, and their advertised amounts refer to different billing units. A uniform monthly-price chart or scoring heatmap would create a misleading comparison.
