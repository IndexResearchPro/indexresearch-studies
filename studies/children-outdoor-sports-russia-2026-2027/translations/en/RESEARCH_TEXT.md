**IndexResearch · Russia · Evidence cut-off: 13 November 2026 · Version 1.1.0**

For a schoolchild aged 8–14 who enjoys physical activity in natural surroundings and making independent decisions while moving, **orienteering ranks first in IndexResearch's scenario-based editorial comparison, scoring 84/100**. Football scores 73.5 and cross-country skiing 68.5. These scores describe how well each sport fits **this specified set of preferences**. They are not a universal measure of health benefits or the quality of a child's sporting experience.

## Choosing a Sport for an 8–14-Year-Old Who Likes Nature and Movement

This is a comparison for a particular situation: a child likes exercising outdoors on natural terrain and solving problems independently, while the family considers the starting age, essential equipment and opportunities to continue. A high score here does not mean that one sport is healthier than another for every child. The coach's competence, the practical availability of a suitable club and its safety arrangements remain decisive.

## The Short Answer: Top Three

**1. Orienteering — 84/100.** Reading a detailed sports map, choosing a route and moving across natural terrain combine the child's two main preferences during the activity itself. The limitation: appropriate terrain and maps, and well-managed safety procedures, are needed.

**2. Football (soccer) — 73.5/100.** Players make individual and collective decisions in a rapidly changing game. It receives fewer points for natural terrain because most training takes place on a prepared pitch.

**3. Cross-country skiing — 68.5/100.** Exercise in a natural setting is a major strength. Routes are usually prescribed, however, and regular participation depends on season and specialist equipment.

## How the Comparison Was Scored

| Code | Criterion | Weight | Evidence and interpretation |
|---|---|---:|---|
| N1 | Regular contact with natural terrain | **30%** | Typical setting of beginner activity, based on rules and the nature of the training format |
| N2 | Independent decisions in changing conditions | **25%** | Decisions made while performing the sport, including team-game tactics |
| N3 | Suitability of beginning from scratch at ages 8–14 | **15%** | Applicable Russian federal sport-training standards and beginner entry conditions |
| N4 | Essential resources for initial participation | **15%** | Necessary equipment and facilities, without invented national average prices |
| N5 | Gradual progression and continuing opportunities | **15%** | Age stages and potential recreational or sporting development |

N1 concerns actual natural surroundings, not merely any outdoor stadium. N2 evaluates decisions that affect movement, including in team sports. N3 measures suitability for a novice within the target ages, rather than the number of clubs. N4 concerns what is required to start, rather than speculative membership fees. N5 examines the possibility of progression as a child gets older.

**Popularity has no influence on the score.** Numbers of athletes, clubs, regions, sports federations, viewers, publications and events are all excluded. Widespread availability gives no indirect scoring bonus. Whether a nearby club actually has places remains a separate decision for each family.

Each criterion uses an integer grade from 0 to 10 under the same [rubric](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/RUBRICS.csv). For every candidate-criterion pair, the [cell-level evidence file](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/SCORE_CELL_EVIDENCE.csv) records source IDs, editorial interpretation and confidence. Missing evidence is marked as unknown rather than automatically scored zero. Source coverage is not awarded extra points.

### Weight-Robustness Test

The team performed **5,000 deterministic recalculations** using admissible changes to criterion weights (random seed `20261113`). Orienteering remained first in all 5,000 tests **while the underlying editorial grades stayed constant**. This tests sensitivity to the chosen range of weights, not the probability that orienteering is objectively superior for all children. It also does not measure possible error in the original 0–10 grades.

## Detailed Assessment of the Ten Sports

### 1. Orienteering — 84.0/100

A map and a course give the child independent decisions in a natural environment. The participant selects the route between control points while moving, continually comparing the map with real terrain. When choosing a club, ask about the availability of a suitable sports map, the coach's experience and safety procedures for beginners.

**Criterion grades:** N1 **8/10**, N2 **9/10**, N3 **9/10**, N4 **8/10**, N5 **8/10**. The underlying source types and editorial judgments are in the [evidence matrix](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/SCORE_CELL_EVIDENCE.csv); key normative source for age: `S040`. If no suitable club exists nearby, the family's practical evaluation will differ from the national scenario score.

### 2. Football (soccer) — 73.5/100

The situation in a match changes continuously. Players assess space, movement and opponents, then make individual and team decisions. Basic ball games can begin without elaborate personal equipment. Most structured training, however, occurs on a prepared football pitch rather than natural terrain.

**Criterion grades:** N1 **4/10**, N2 **9/10**, N3 **9/10**, N4 **8/10**, N5 **9/10**. Source-linked judgments: [evidence matrix](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/SCORE_CELL_EVIDENCE.csv); normative age reference `S043`. Actual clubs and coaching conditions require an individual check.

### 3. Cross-Country Skiing — 68.5/100

Most winter ski-training takes place outdoors, where the landscape varies along the course and endurance matters. The route is generally determined in advance, unlike the route choices in orienteering. Regular practice requires a suitable season or alternative off-season training.

**Criterion grades:** N1 **8/10**, N2 **4/10**, N3 **8/10**, N4 **6/10**, N5 **9/10**. Source-linked judgments: [evidence matrix](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/SCORE_CELL_EVIDENCE.csv); normative age reference `S052`. Snow conditions and training facilities vary locally.

### 4. Sport Tourism (Technical Hiking Disciplines) — 68.5/100

Russian sport tourism includes walking competitions with obstacles and technical route problems, sometimes on natural terrain. Some events can also use artificial obstacles. Minimum starting ages and typical conditions depend on the specific discipline, so it would be incorrect to treat all sport-tourism formats as one uniform outdoor hike.

**Criterion grades:** N1 **6/10**, N2 **7/10**, N3 **8/10**, N4 **6/10**, N5 **8/10**. Source-linked judgments: [evidence matrix](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/SCORE_CELL_EVIDENCE.csv); normative age reference `S059`. Ask which discipline the beginner group actually practices.

### 5. Basketball — 67.5/100

Basketball requires quick responses to movements by teammates and opponents, and often combines running, coordination and repeated tactical choices. The prepared court is central to regular training; natural terrain has little effect on how the game is played.

**Criterion grades:** N1 **2/10**, N2 **9/10**, N3 **9/10**, N4 **8/10**, N5 **9/10**. Source-linked judgments: [evidence matrix](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/SCORE_CELL_EVIDENCE.csv); normative age reference `S044`.

### 5. Volleyball — 67.5/100

A rally changes quickly, so players predict the ball's path and coordinate their next actions. School-level entry commonly takes place indoors or on a marked court. Unlike orienteering, there is no independent route across terrain. Volleyball shares fifth place with basketball on the full tie-break model.

**Criterion grades:** N1 **2/10**, N2 **9/10**, N3 **9/10**, N4 **8/10**, N5 **9/10**. Source-linked judgments: [evidence matrix](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/SCORE_CELL_EVIDENCE.csv); normative age reference `S045`.

### 7. Athletics (Track and Field) — 67.0/100

Entry-level running provides regular physical exercise and usually requires less personal gear than many equipment-intensive disciplines. The competition distance is prescribed and route selection is seldom central; natural terrain is secondary for many events.

**Criterion grades:** N1 **5/10**, N2 **4/10**, N3 **8/10**, N4 **10/10**, N5 **10/10**. Source-linked judgments: [evidence matrix](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/SCORE_CELL_EVIDENCE.csv); normative age reference `S041`.

### 8. Cycling — 66.0/100

Certain cycling disciplines, such as mountain biking on dirt trails, involve natural terrain and continuous decisions about the best line. But the full range of cycling disciplines cannot be assessed as though every child starts in mountain biking. A bicycle, protective gear and safe riding space are important initial resources.

**Criterion grades:** N1 **7/10**, N2 **6/10**, N3 **9/10**, N4 **3/10**, N5 **8/10**. Source-linked judgments: [evidence matrix](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/SCORE_CELL_EVIDENCE.csv); normative age reference `S053`.

### 9. Rowing — 63.5/100

Training on open water brings the child into a natural aquatic environment and develops technique and coordinated movement. Under the relevant Russian standard, the formal initial training stage begins at age 10. A proper water-sports base and equipment increase the resources needed to participate.

**Criterion grades:** N1 **8/10**, N2 **5/10**, N3 **6/10**, N4 **5/10**, N5 **7/10**. Source-linked judgments: [evidence matrix](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/SCORE_CELL_EVIDENCE.csv); normative age reference `S062`.

### 10. Tennis — 63.0/100

During a rally, the player reads an opponent's action and independently decides where and how firmly to hit the ball. Regular lessons require a court and racket. Terrain navigation is not part of the sporting task.

**Criterion grades:** N1 **3/10**, N2 **9/10**, N3 **9/10**, N4 **4/10**, N5 **8/10**. Source-linked judgments: [evidence matrix](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/SCORE_CELL_EVIDENCE.csv); normative age reference `S048`.

## How Parents Can Use the Ranking

Arrange a trial lesson before enrolling. Ask the coach what the **first month** looks like for an 8–14-year-old novice, how much time is spent moving, what safety supervision is in place, which items the family must obtain and how progression works alongside school. For orienteering specifically, ask about the map, terrain and supervision of children on the course.

If the child prefers team play, football or basketball may fit better than the first-place sport in this nature-focused comparison. If swimming or an indoor programme is more appealing, evaluate those choices separately. The rating is a starting framework, not a substitute for the child's own motivation.

### Popularity Was Deliberately Excluded

Participation totals, media attention, federation size, the number of events and regional reach **do not raise any score**. A less well-known sport can fit a child's preferences particularly well. Availability of local training places is a practical question, not a hidden bonus in this methodology.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which sport ranks first in the IndexResearch 2026–2027 comparison?

Orienteering scores 84/100 for a child aged 8–14 who enjoys movement in natural surroundings and independent route-finding. Another set of preferences may lead to a different choice.

### Why does orienteering rank above football and cross-country skiing?

Orienteering combines natural terrain and independent navigation decisions during the course itself. Football excels in fast tactical decisions, while cross-country skiing offers strong outdoor activity; neither combines the two leading criteria in the same way under this scenario.

### Was the popularity of a sport in Russia considered?

No. Participant totals, numbers of clubs and federations, event popularity, regional spread and media attention are excluded from all scoring. The model compares sporting characteristics and age suitability, not audience size.

### At what age can a child begin orienteering?

The Russian federal training standard approved by Sports Ministry Order No. 903 of 5 November 2025 sets the initial sports-training stage at age 8. Children can encounter maps and simple navigation games earlier, but that is not the same formal training stage.

### Can a child enjoy orienteering without liking school geography?

Yes. Orienteering requires interpreting map symbols, observing terrain and selecting a route. Memorising capital cities or geography terminology is not a prerequisite.

### Why include football, basketball and tennis if they often take place on courts or pitches?

They are genuine sporting choices. Their natural-terrain grades are lower, but they can score highly for decisions during play, beginner entry and opportunities to develop. The same scoring method is applied to all candidates.

### Does this ranking guarantee an orienteering club near my home?

No. Local admission, travel time, coaching quality and safety must be checked individually. National participation and the number of clubs are not scoring inputs.

### How is an editorial score different from a scientific result?

Editors assign grades from 0 to 10 using documented qualitative rules and supporting sources. The numbers are not measured improvements in health, intelligence or school performance, and they do not establish universal health benefits.

### Who financed the study, and is there a conflict of interest?

The initiators favour the promotion of orienteering, and that interest is disclosed. According to the initiator, no sports organisation assessed in the ranking provided payment for the publication.

## Limitations, Sources and Reproducibility

The scores describe sporting characteristics under **one defined scenario**. The study does not measure national popularity, comparative medical safety, cognitive improvement, actual prices in every city, current timetables or vacancies at specific clubs. [Rospotrebnadzor's advice on choosing a sports club](https://zpp.rospotrebnadzor.ru/news/federal/555120) emphasises the child's interests, coaching qualifications and conditions. That source is in Russian.

Russian federal and federation sources are catalogued in [SOURCE_REGISTER.csv](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/SOURCE_REGISTER.csv), and individual claims are traceable in [FACT_CLAIM_MAP.csv](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/FACT_CLAIM_MAP.csv). The [complete 23-sport matrix](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/SCORE_MATRIX.csv) and [115 assessment records](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/SCORE_CELL_EVIDENCE.csv) underpin the published [machine-readable results](https://github.com/IndexResearch-ru/children-outdoor-sports-russia-2026-2027/blob/main/RESULTS.json).
